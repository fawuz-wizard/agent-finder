# Design: Agent Dashboard, the first screen

## Problem Statement

The agent's first screen carries everything the app knows: presence pills, two "customers now see" cards, three figures, a float line, alert sentences, a three-line chart with four ranges, and the day's timeline. An agent opens this screen twenty times a day. Most of it is scrolled past, and the thing only this app can tell them, "am I visible to customers right now?", is one block among eight.

## What Makes This Cool

The top of the screen is the agent's own listing in the finder's result-card frame: the orange-edged card with the shop's name, its street, and the green or amber pill for each of the two sides a customer could ask for. Under it, Open / Away / Closed. Tap Away and the card greys out. Cause and effect on one screen, and the one view nobody else gives an agent. (A customer sees one pill for their own request, plus distance and "Get details"; this card shows both sides and no distance, so it is "in the customer's frame", not a pixel copy.)

## Constraints

- The app opens the agent by their working hours; the first screen's job is to show visibility and let them switch OFF or Away, not to make them switch on each morning.
- Customers never see figures; the agent's first screen shows no balance or float figure either. The float request appears as a state (pending, approved, declined), never an amount on the first screen.
- Transactions are recorded for Orange's stakeholders, not for the competition; they stay on Services.
- Same design language as the customer module (Poppins, dark surface, the finder's pieces).

## Premises (agreed)

1. Visibility first: the agent is auto-open by hours, so the screen answers "am I seen?" and offers OFF.
2. Money figures stay off the first screen; float shows as a state.
3. Alerts appear only when there is one, as red-edged rows under the status pills. No empty alert box.
4. The chart and the timeline move to their own tab, Activity. Four tabs (Dashboard, Activity, Services, Profile) spread the same information so each screen is short; nothing is removed. That is where Orange and the judges see the evidence, one tap from anywhere.

## Approaches Considered

- **A: Status card with pills.** Keep the pills, drop the chart and timeline, add "My numbers ›". Smallest change; reads as settings, not as the customer's view. Not chosen.
- **B: Your own listing, live.** The finder's result card for this shop on top, the switch under it. Chosen.
- **C: Urgency feed.** Cards reordered by what matters most today. Short on quiet days; loses muscle memory. Not chosen.

## Recommended Approach: B, your own listing, live

Dashboard, top to bottom:

1. **Title row** "Dashboard" and the pin row (shop name, code · area, "Hours" action, hours line). Closing warning as an orange-edged card only when it applies, with "Stay open 1 more hour".
2. **Your listing.** Caption "CUSTOMERS NOW SEE" (the existing wording; tests select it). A new `ListingCard` on the `EdgeCard` frame (which gains a `muted` prop for the greyed edge), capped at: shop name (`home.name`), street (`home.area`, which the API fills from the street), one status pill per side (`customers_see.sides`, label and phrase), then two muted lines: the ranges line ("Cash out: any amount · Deposit: up to SLE 10,000", each range in its own `<span>`; agent-only, never shown to customers) and the existing `customers_see.explanation` sentence (which carries the operator-feed wording the tests assert). The side's `why` shows as a warning line when present. `above_text` and `estimate_text` leave the Dashboard. The caption and the card sit in one wrapping `div`, as the customers-see test expects. The ranges line may show the agent's own serving ceiling ("up to SLE 8,347" under the operator feed): it is the agent's own limit, already shown to them today, not a balance and not the aggregator's figure; the "no money figure" constraint is about balances and float positions.
   **Greyed** means: `data-state` set to the `customers_see.state` (`hidden`, `closed`, `expired`, `unlocated`), the pills replaced by the headline (`customers_see.headline`) and its explanation, the name in the muted colour, the orange edge replaced by the muted colour. Applies to all four non-open states; `expired` also raises its attention sentence, and both show. Unlocated adds a "Pin my shop" pill inside the card.
   Each side's pill colour comes from a new `outcome: 'likely' | 'limited' | 'not_set' | 'unknown'` field on `CustomersSeeSide` (typed as the public outcome union, since the API passes `public_outcome()` through and can also emit `unknown`; anything but `likely` draws amber), set in `apps/api/app/services/phrasing.py` (which builds `customers_see`; the customer search does not consume this dict) and in the demo alike; `ServiceStatus` already accepts these values, with anything but `likely` drawn amber. Today the screen guesses from a string compare that the demo never matches.
3. **Are you open?** Three 50 px pills: Open, Away, Closed. One line under: the presence label and, when the operator feed is on, its source sentence. A failed save shows its error under the pills (`role="alert"`), as today. `night_mode` passes through unchanged on save; there is no control for it on this screen.
4. **Alerts**, only when present: each attention sentence as a red-edged row.
5. **Today.** One 60 px row with three figures: found you, transactions (the operator's count, or "logged by you" when no feed), reported a problem. Logging itself stays on Services; only the count shows here. No link row: the Activity tab is one tap away in the bar.
6. **Float.** One 60 px row, always present: "Float request" with the state on the right, linking to Services. No amount. The home payload gains `latest_float` (the most recent request, any state, or null) on the API and the demo; `pending_float` stays for older callers. Row text by state:

   | `latest_float` | Row text |
   |---|---|
   | null, or finished (completed, declined or cancelled) more than 24 h ago | Request float › |
   | pending | Pending › |
   | approved (any age; it is still in progress) | Approved › |
   | completed, within 24 h of `decided_at` | Completed › |
   | declined, within 24 h of `decided_at` | Declined › |
   | cancelled, within 24 h | Request float › |

Loading ("Loading your day…"), the error branch with "Try again", and the pin row's `ref · area` are unchanged from today.

Gone from the Dashboard: the chart and its range tabs, the "Your numbers for…" heading, the timeline, the footer note. All but the footer note move to the Numbers screen. The explanation sentence moves into the listing card (see block 2).

The pin row (block 1) and the listing card (block 2) both show the shop name and the street, on purpose: the pin row is the app's constant header, the same on Dashboard and Profile; the card is the customer's frame, and repeating the name there is what makes it read as "this is what they see".

**New tab: Activity** (`/agent/activity`, second in the bar: Dashboard, Activity, Services, Profile; bar-chart icon). Title row "Activity". The range switch, the "Your numbers for …" heading (the chart panel's accessible name; the chart test looks for it), the existing line "N found you (+d vs before) · status fresh X% of your open hours" (wording unchanged, so the re-pointed chart test passes as is), the chart, then the timeline. Unchanged content, new home. The old `/agent/activity` redirect to the Dashboard is replaced by this screen.

**Services and Profile** are untouched by this design.

## Open Questions

- "Not right?" under the listing (the agent's one honest correction: low on cash / low on float today) was agreed in principle earlier; it is not on this first screen yet. Add once the estimate logic treats it as evidence.
- Whether Away should auto-expire (back after N minutes) or stay until changed. Nothing auto-expires today; the "back after 25 minutes" line in the demo is a seed timeline row, not behaviour.

## Success Criteria

- The first screen fits in two phone heights with no alert and no closing warning; the tab bar shows four tabs with the active one orange.
- A new agent can say, within ten seconds of opening it, whether customers can find them right now.
- Switching to Away visibly changes the listing card without leaving the screen.
- The chart, ranges and timeline are all on the Activity tab, one tap from anywhere, unchanged.
- Existing tests for presence, closing warning and the operator feed pass unchanged; the customers-see test passes because each range keeps its own element; the chart test is re-pointed at the Numbers screen. New tests: Away greys the listing (`data-state="hidden"`, headline shown, no pills); the float row text per state; the Numbers route renders the chart.

## What I noticed about how you think

- "if you don't switch it off by morning you are already seen": you think in defaults, not in taps. That one sentence set premise 1 and removed a whole morning ritual from the app.
- "problems alert is valid also because with that it could caution their behaviour": you want the app to change what agents do, not just inform them. Alerts stay at the top for that reason.
- You chose to move the chart rather than delete it, while saying you were "beginning to think we should remove it". Keeping the evidence one tap away is the right instinct for an app that has to impress Orange and serve agents at the same time.
